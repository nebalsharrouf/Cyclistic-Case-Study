📋 Project Overview

Cyclistic is a fictional bike‑share company based in Chicago. The director of marketing believes the company’s future success depends on maximizing the number of annual memberships. Therefore, your team wants to understand how casual riders and annual members use Cyclistic bikes differently.

This case study follows the six phases of data analysis: Ask, Prepare, Process, Analyze, Share, and Act. By combining historical trip data with R and Tableau, we derive actionable insights to convert casual riders into members.

Scroll down or use the navigation to explore each phase.

1. Ask

Business Task: Identify key differences in bike usage between casual riders and annual members to inform a new marketing strategy aimed at converting casual riders into members.

Stakeholders: Cyclistic executive team, marketing analytics team, and the director of marketing, Lily Moreno.

2. Prepare

Data Source: Cyclistic’s historical trip data from the past 12 months (provided by Motivate International Inc. under license).

Credibility & Integrity: The data is stored in a secure, internal database and is considered reliable (ROCCC). It includes anonymous trip details such as start/end time, station names, and rider type.

3. Process

Data Cleaning & Transformation: Using R (tidyverse) to combine 12 CSV files, remove duplicates, filter nulls, and create ride length, day of week, month. Checked for outliers.

Tools: R, RStudio, dplyr, tidyr, lubridate.

4. Analyze

Key Findings: Casual riders take longer rides and are more active on weekends (leisure). Members have shorter, consistent rides on weekdays (commuting). Peak hours differ.

5. Share

Visualizations: Tableau dashboards showing ride counts by day, ride length histograms. Presented via slide deck and report.

6. Act

Recommendations:

  • Weekend marketing campaign for casual riders highlighting membership savings.
  • Introduce "weekend-only" or off-peak annual pass.
  • App feature comparing trip costs vs. membership.
