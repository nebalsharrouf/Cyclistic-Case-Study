🚴 Cyclistic Bike-Share: A Data Analysis Case Study

Ask Prepare Process Analyze Share Act

1. Ask

Business Task: Identify key differences in bike usage between casual riders and annual members to inform a new marketing strategy aimed at converting casual riders into members.

Stakeholders: Cyclistic executive team, marketing analytics team, and the director of marketing, Lily Moreno.

2. Prepare

Data Source: Cyclistic’s historical trip data from the past 12 months (provided by Motivate International Inc. under license).

Credibility & Integrity: The data is stored in a secure, internal database and is considered reliable (ROCCC: Reliable, Original, Comprehensive, Current, Cited). It includes anonymous trip details such as start/end time, station names, and rider type (casual vs. member).

3. Process

Data Cleaning & Transformation: Using R (with tidyverse) to combine 12 CSV files, remove duplicates, filter out null values, and create new calculated fields such as ride length, day of week, and month for deeper analysis. Data was also checked for outliers and inconsistencies.

Tools Used: R, RStudio, and the `dplyr`, `tidyr`, and `lubridate` packages.

4. Analyze

Key Findings: Analysis revealed that casual riders tend to take longer rides and are more active on weekends, suggesting leisure use. Annual members, on the other hand, have shorter, more consistent rides throughout the workweek, indicating commuting or utilitarian use. Peak hours also differ significantly.

5. Share

Visualizations & Communication: Created interactive dashboards using Tableau Public to clearly illustrate usage patterns. Key visualizations include bar charts comparing ride counts by day of week and histograms of ride lengths. These findings will be presented to stakeholders via a slide deck and a written report.

6. Act

Recommendations:

  • Launch a targeted weekend marketing campaign for casual riders, highlighting the cost-saving benefits of membership for regular leisure use.
  • Introduce a new "weekend-only" or "off-peak" annual pass tier.
  • Develop a mobile app feature that allows casual riders to easily compare their trip costs against membership options after each ride.
